## FAQ
### How much will my detail cost?
The example packages show starting prices. For a real service, size, condition and agreed scope determine the final quotation before work begins.
### How long will it take?
Example timings range from 2–3 hours for an exterior detail to 2–3 days for ceramic protection. These are planning estimates rather than guaranteed completion times.
### Do you offer mobile detailing?
This concept is designed for studio appointments only.
### Which service should I choose?
Select “Not sure — advise me” in the demo form if you would like guidance.
### Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?
No. This portfolio form demonstrates a request flow and does not create or send a real booking.
### Can an appointment be changed or cancelled?
The fictional business policy asks for at least 24 hours’ notice. No actual appointment is created in this demo.
### Will polishing remove every scratch?
No. Suitability depends on defect depth and paint condition. The concept does not promise complete scratch removal.
